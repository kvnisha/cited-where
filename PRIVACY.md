# Privacy

Cited where? collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own.

- **What it downloads:** only from arxiv.org. That is the PDF you open and, occasionally,
  the paper's LaTeX source (used to match an arXiv ID to an author–year reference).
- **Where it goes:** everything is processed inside your browser and kept in memory.
  Nothing is written to disk, and it is discarded when you close the tab.
- **What it can see:** its permissions are limited to arxiv.org. It cannot read other
  websites you visit.
- **What it sends:** nothing, to anyone.
